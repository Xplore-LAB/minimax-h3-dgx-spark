# MiniMax H3 测试提示词模板

## 1. 工业场景稳定性测试

```text
A fixed medium-wide shot inside a modern air separation plant control room.
Two engineers in standard safety clothing inspect a process trend display.
The camera remains stable. Realistic industrial ambience, low ventilation hum,
subtle keyboard sounds, natural speech-free background, no subtitles, no logos,
no on-screen text.
```

用途：

- 测试人物、显示屏、工业环境和稳定镜头；
- 避免字幕与标牌导致文字伪影；
- 检查环境音是否自然。

## 2. 设备运动测试

```text
Close-up of a stainless-steel cryogenic valve actuator slowly opening in a
clean industrial plant. Condensation is visible on insulated piping. The camera
makes a slow controlled dolly movement. Realistic mechanical actuator sound,
soft plant ventilation, no people, no speech, no text, no watermark.
```

用途：

- 测试机械运动连续性；
- 检查局部结构是否漂移；
- 检查动作声和视觉是否同步。

## 3. 科研演示测试

```text
A clean laboratory tabletop demonstration of a miniature distillation column.
Colored indicator lights change gradually while a researcher adjusts one knob.
Static camera, realistic materials, soft room ambience, subtle equipment hum,
no dialogue, no captions, no watermark.
```

## 4. 通用结构

```text
[镜头类型与景别] + [场景] + [主体] + [动作] + [摄影机运动]
+ [光照与材质] + [环境声音] + [限制项]
```

建议限制项：

```text
no subtitles, no on-screen text, no watermark, no logo,
stable geometry, realistic motion, coherent audio
```

## 5. 调试原则

- 首轮只描述一个主动作；
- 不同时要求复杂对白、快速运镜和多人交互；
- 同一基准测试固定提示词与 Seed；
- 先用英文提示词建立性能基线；
- 画面稳定后再测试中文、对白和复杂音频。
